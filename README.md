# Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is a Tableau dashboard created to analyze sales and profit performance.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit
- Compare yearly sales and profit
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Compare sales by region
- Analyze category-wise sales and profit
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze profit margin

## Tools Used
- Tableau
- Excel / CSV
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## Dashboards
### Dashboard 1 – Sales & Profit Analysis
This dashboard provides:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales & Profit by Year
- Sales by Region
- Category-wise Sales & Profit

### Dashboard 2 – Product Performance & Profitability
This dashboard provides:
- Top N Products
- Sales vs Profit Analysis
- Category & Sub-Category Performance
- Top Profitable Products
- Top Products by Profit Margin
- Performance Quadrant Analysis

## Key Insights
- Sales increased in 2024 compared to 2023.
- South region generated the highest sales.
- Overall profit margin is 16%.
- Laptop Model 19 is one of the top profitable products.
- High Sales–High Profit products perform strongly.
